PRODUCT:
Internal assistant for founder-led market penetration.

PRIMARY USER:
Founder / technical operator of ATI.

SECONDARY USERS:
None (single-user system by design).

---

USER GOALS:
- Find the *right* people (infra / AI platform engineers)
- Engage only when there is real pain
- Sound technical, calm, and credible
- Avoid marketing tone entirely
- Maintain personal voice while scaling attention

---

USER EXPERIENCE (DAILY FLOW):

1. User opens the system (CLI or simple web UI)
2. Sees a queue of “High-Signal Opportunities”
3. Clicks one item
4. Sees:
   - Source (GitHub issue, LinkedIn post, etc.)
   - Why it is relevant
   - Risk score (spam / irrelevance)
   - Recommended channel action
   - 2–3 drafted response variants
5. User:
   - Approves
   - Edits
   - Rejects
6. System logs outcome and applies cooldown rules

---

WEEKLY FLOW:

- Weekly digest:
  - Top recurring failure modes observed
  - Which frameworks show most pain
  - Which messages got responses

---

UX CONSTRAINTS:
- No dashboards full of charts
- Text-first
- Bias toward review and judgment
- Everything skimmable in <5 minutes

---

OUT OF SCOPE (v1):
- CRM
- Analytics attribution
- Automated scheduling
- Multi-user roles
