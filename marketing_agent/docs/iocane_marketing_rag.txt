================================================================
COMPANY DEFINITION
================================================================

Company Name: iocane

Short Definition:
iocane is the reliability platform for agentic systems.

Extended Definition:
iocane builds reliability infrastructure for AI agent architectures,
helping teams observe, explain, and prevent coordination failures
before and during production.

iocane focuses specifically on agent-native failure modes
that emerge from multi-agent coordination, not generic application monitoring.

================================================================
CORE PROBLEM IOCANE SOLVES
================================================================

Modern AI systems are increasingly agentic:
- multi-step
- concurrent
- tool-calling
- stateful
- distributed across models and services

These systems fail in ways traditional observability, APM,
and AIOps tools do not understand.

Common agentic failure modes include:
- fan-out collapse
- retry storms
- token starvation
- queue/backpressure cascades
- tool-call contention and rate-limit amplification
- tail-latency explosions due to coordination bottlenecks

These failures:
- are hard to diagnose
- are often triggered by seemingly “safe” changes
- lead to outages, degraded UX, and runaway cost

iocane exists to make agentic systems safe to scale.

================================================================
PRODUCT SUITE OVERVIEW
================================================================

iocane is a multi-product company focused on agentic reliability.

------------------------------------------------
1. iocane-ATI (Agent Telemetry & Intelligence)
------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
Runtime observability and diagnostics for agentic systems.

Primary Question Answered:
“What is breaking in my agent system right now — and why?”

Core Capabilities:
- agent-native OpenTelemetry instrumentation
- detection of coordination pathologies
- explainable failure diagnostics
- actionable mitigation guidance
- policy hooks for runtime safeguards

iocane-ATI operates during production and incidents.

------------------------------------------------
2. iocane-CIA (Change Impact Analyzer)
------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
Pre-change and rollout-time impact analysis for agentic systems.

Primary Question Answered:
“If I change this agent system, what will break — and how do I prevent it?”

Core Capabilities:
- analyzes proposed agent graph, policy, and runtime changes
- predicts coordination failure risks before deployment
- forecasts which agentic failure detectors are likely to trigger
- estimates impact on latency, failure rate, and cost
- produces rollout and mitigation recommendations

iocane-CIA operates before and during change events.

------------------------------------------------
Relationship Between Products
------------------------------------------------

iocane-ATI + iocane-CIA together cover the full reliability lifecycle:

- Before change: predict and prevent failures (CIA)
- During runtime: detect and explain failures (ATI)

Both products share:
- agent-native semantics
- the same buyer persona
- the same failure taxonomy
- the same reliability narrative
